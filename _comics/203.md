If you were in trouble, the Coast Guard would show up in the dead of night, in a storm to end all storms, knowing they would probably die, just to save you and your family. Please - if you're not impacted by the shutdown, donate a few dollars to Coast Guard Mutual Assistance. Organizations like this are only source of relief our brothers and sisters in arms have right now. They're working for free at the moment, for you.
