The Coast Guard claims August 4th, 1790 as its founding date, but we could just as easily claim August 7th, 1789 - the creation of the Lighthouse Service. If you think that makes us less of a military branch, remember that George Washington thought lighthouses were so dangerous during the Revolution that he had them dismantled
