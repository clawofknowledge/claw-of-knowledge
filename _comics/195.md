When you think about it, the different military branches are all like one big family getting together around the table for Thanksgiving: while we each have our own traditions and our ways of doing things, we still find a way to sit down together and bicker endlessly about which service is really the best.

Happy Thanksgiving, brothers and sisters in arms!
