On March 9th, 1944, the Coast Guard-manned destroyer-escort USS LEOPOLD was torpedoed south of Iceland. The ship split in half, killing 171 Coasties and leaving only 28 survivors. She is the Coast Guard's largest single loss of life in combat, and the first destroyer-escort sunk during the war

Something to remember this Memorial Day
