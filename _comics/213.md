Despite what Task & Purpose and Military.com may say, the Quentin Walsh isn't the first ship the Navy named after a Coastie. Why? Because we've been kicking ass for centuries.

EDIT: I didn't mean this to be an exhaustive list, but fans have pointed out I'm missing USS REDNOUR (APD-102), named for Forrest Rednour, who rescued survivors of the sinking of the USS DORCHESTER, and two destroyers named after Charles Satterlee, CO of the TAMPA. Let me know if you know of more!
