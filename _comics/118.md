At Normandy on June 6th, 1944, the Coast Guard manned roughly a hundred ships and boats, ranging from landing craft to the operation's flagship, the USS Bayfield. Not only that, but they saved the lives of over 400 soldiers and sailors stranded on the waves under enemy fire.
